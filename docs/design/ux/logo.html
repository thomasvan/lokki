Lokki — the mark

“lokki” is Finnish for seagull. A gull in flight, reduced to two wing curves and the small point where they meet. Every drawing on this page is made for Lokki.

01

The mark

At 512, 128, 32 and 16 px, in the structure colour on light and dark. The 512 artboard is outlined so its edge is visible.

02

Construction

The mark ships as ui/src/components/Mark.svelte: one filled path on a 64 grid, no text, filter, gradient or image.

One solid shape, so it stays a gull from 512 px down to a 16 px menu bar and works as a template image with nothing to fill in.

grid64 × 64, wings span 4 → 60 wingtip angle ≈ 30°, shoulder 8 units thick bodythe wings meet in a point 8 units deep 16 pxredrawn heavier for the tray and favicon
03

App icon

The mark in white over a dusk sky with a sea band, on a continuous-corner square at macOS proportions (an 824 body on a 1024 canvas). At 32 and 16 px the gull is swapped for its 16 px drawing so each wing keeps a full pixel.

04

Wordmark

“Lokki”, a proper noun, drawn as paths so it needs no font and reads the same on every platform. The letters are centrelines stroked in the ink; the dot of the i is in the accent: the gull at 64 px and up, the gull’s 16 px drawing from 28 px, and a plain dot below, where a gull of 2 px does not read. The mark stands beside it as the sidebar sets it.

05

Tray

Template images: black on transparent, shape only; the OS tints them for the menu bar. Solid while the relay is cutting; the line, the same wings with no fill, while requests pass through. An outline of the solid wings blurs to grey at 16 px, so the open form is a 1.5 px line on the 16 px grid; the 32 px row is the @2x.

06

Favicon

For the browser tab. Full-bleed tile; at 16 px the sea band is dropped and the gull fills the width.
