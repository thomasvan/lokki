lokki — three drawn wordmarks

Today the wordmark is the text “lokki” in the system face at 600. Each direction below is drawn as SVG paths, so it needs no font and stays offline. Each is shown at 64, 28 and 15 px on light and dark, beside the mark as the sidebar places it, and inside the 208 px sidebar. In the app the wordmark appears only in the sidebar, at 15 px.

00

Today

The text wordmark, for comparison: system face, 600, tracking from −0.035em at 64 px to −0.01em at 15 px. It renders in whatever face the platform supplies, so it looks different on macOS, Windows and Linux.

01

A · “Lokki”

A rounded geometric sans with a capital L, heavier than today’s 600, set tight. Every stroke is a centreline with round caps; the k’s arm meets the stem low and the leg branches from the arm.

At 15 px
Reads as a word at 1× and 2×: 11 px to the top of the L, a 7.5 px x-height, stems about 2 px, so it is as heavy as the 26 px gull beside it. The counter of the o stays open. Set on the same em as today’s text it looks a size smaller than the system face, whose x-height is larger; the sidebar can set it at 16 or 17 px without changing the drawing. The capital makes it the only place the name is not lower case; the window title, the docs and the error page say “lokki”.
Paths
2: one stroked path of 9 strokes (L, o, two k’s of three strokes each, the i’s stem) and one filled dot.
To keep
Cheap. The weight is one number (the stroke width) and the tracking is where each stroke starts, so a heavier, lighter or looser version is an edit, not a redraw; a different letter is one new centreline. In the UI it is a Wordmark.svelte beside Mark.svelte, inside the sidebar’s h1 with role="img" and aria-label="lokki", so the heading keeps its name. The error page has no wordmark today; giving it one is a second copy of the path, because that page is not built from the UI.
02

B · “Lokki”, the gull as the dot

As A, with the dot of the i in the structure colour: the gull at 64 px, the gull’s 16 px drawing at 28 px (the rule the app icon follows at 32 px and under), and a plain dot at 15 px, where a 2 px gull does not read.

At 15 px
The same as A with a coloured dot, and at 1× the dot’s colour barely shows. Since the app shows the wordmark only at 15 px, the gull-dot never appears in the app: it lives where the wordmark is drawn large, such as a README or a release page. Beside the mark at 28 and 64 px the gull appears twice.
Paths
2 at each size, in three drawings: A’s stroked path, plus the gull (64), the 16 px gull (28) or the dot (15).
To keep
A’s cost plus the mark’s: the dot is a copy of the mark at two sizes, so a change to the mark is three places to change, and the size at which the dot switches drawing is one more rule to keep.
03

C · “lokki”, lettered

Lower case, drawn as one piece of lettering: the l and the i end in the same tail, and the first k’s leg runs on into the stem of the second, so the double k is one stroke. A little lighter than A, as lettering wants.

At 15 px
Reads at 1× and 2×, and stays lower case like the name. At 1× the join of the k’s blurs into one dark v at the baseline, so the double k reads as a block; at 2× the join shows. The tails shrink to a pixel but still keep the l from reading as an I. Like A, it looks a size smaller than today’s text on the same em.
Paths
2: one stroked path of 6 strokes (l, o, the first k’s stem, the first k’s arm and leg running into the second stem, the second k’s arm and leg, the i) and one filled dot.
To keep
The weight is still one number, but the k’s are tied: moving either k, or changing the space between them, means redrawing the joining stroke. A change of letter means re-lettering the neighbours so the tails and the join still agree. In the UI and the error page it sits as A does.
