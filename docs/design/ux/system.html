Scheme

lokki visual system

Sea and sky, quiet. A deep blue-grey carries structure; teal means the relay is cutting; amber means a request passes through at full price.

tokens.css · 0.1
system-ui · ui-monospace
01

Palette

Ratios are WCAG 2 contrast against --bg unless named. A state's plain token is for dots, bars and icons; its -ink is for text; its -soft is the tint behind both.

02

Type

System faces only. Sentences in system-ui; numbers, reasons, keys and endpoints in ui-monospace, always tabular.

03

Spacing, radii, shadow

A 4 px base. Controls are 32 px tall (28 px in dense rows); cards pad 16 px; sections sit 40 px apart.

--r-sm 4
--r-md 6
--r-lg 10
--r-pill
--shadow-1 · selected segment, active nav
--shadow-2 · popover, tooltip
04

Icons

Drawn for lokki on a 20 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, round caps, currentColor. Shown at 2× over the grid and at size.

05

Mode control

One click changes the mode; no Save. The first control is live. Busy shows while the core confirms; disabled keeps the current mode visible and says why.

06

Decision line

Sits beside the mode control in the top bar of every screen. The headline names the outcome; the reason underneath is quoted verbatim from the core.

07

Buttons

One primary per view. Use the toolbar's Force state to see hover, focus and pressed on every button at once.

08

State chips

Every state colour travels with its word. An account is low in teal because low accounts are what make Auto cut; ok stays neutral; not watched is only an outline.

09

Quota gauge

Remaining quota as a thin bar, with a tick at the 30 % threshold Auto cuts below. The number, the reset phrase and the freshness line sit on the same card.

10

Stat cards and request chart

Specimen with sample counts, not real usage. 24 hourly bars stacked by outcome; the range control sits beside the title; the legend carries the counts.

11

Banners and restart bar

Banners sit above the content they concern. The restart bar is sticky at the top of Settings and stays until CLIProxyAPI restarts — scroll the frame to see it hold.

12

Settings rows

Label and one grey line at left, control at right. A locked row keeps its value readable and gives the reason in place. Each card saves on its own and counts its changes.

13

Sidebar

208 px. Mark and wordmark, four destinations, and the service status at the foot. Hover is forced on Accounts when Force state is set.
